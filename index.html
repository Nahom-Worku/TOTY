About Us

Guided by Faith,
United in Purpose

Toronto Orthodox Tewahedo Youth Fellowship (TOTY) strengthens the spiritual connection of Ethiopian Orthodox youth and young adults through English-based services, education, and community support. Our mission is to guide them back to the church and cultivate knowledgeable, faithful leaders.
